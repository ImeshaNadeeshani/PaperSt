## paperist-campaigns.png — final prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: a single wide triptych campaign illustration for the Paperist stationery website, used behind live HTML text. Reference image is only a style reference: follow its warm hand-drawn manga ink outlines, sunlit Japanese city, paper grain, muted cream with red details and vivid sky blue. Generate one panoramic illustration divided into three equally wide panels, no gaps and no written text anywhere. Left third: a black and white manga close-up of a cheerful young woman looking over the top of her open notebook, intricate ink hatching, white paper background. Middle third: a young woman with a navy school backpack walking in a sunlit Japanese city street, anime linework and watercolor blue sky. Right third: close-up hands offering a cream gift box with a large red ribbon bow, elegant manga linework, warm paper background. All panels must be clean and easily croppable individually, main subjects centred in their own thirds, refined editorial stationery aesthetic. Wide 3:1 composition. No website layout, no typography, no interface elements, no watermark.

## paperist-categories.png — final prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: one single illustrated stationery catalogue contact sheet for Paperist website category tiles. Reference image is a style guide only, not a page to reproduce. Create one precisely aligned 4 column by 2 row grid of 8 equally sized square vignettes with no borders, no gaps, no lettering anywhere, no website UI. Every vignette has an ivory paper background, exquisitely detailed anime manga ink linework with restrained watercolor colors, realistic stationary object proportions, overhead or three-quarter product views and lightly textured paper. Top row from left to right: 1) a red hardback notebook beside a graphite pencil, 2) three elegant black and silver gel pens, 3) an open weekly planner with tiny illegible lines and red bookmarks, 4) a watercolour paint palette and paintbrushes. Bottom row from left to right: 5) a cream desk organizer filled with pens and pencils, 6) a navy school backpack, 7) a small cream gift box tied with a beautiful red ribbon, 8) stacked black and kraft hardcover journals. Objects must be centred in their own cell, not touch adjacent cells, and fill 70% of each square. Same understated warm off-white background in every cell. Match the reference's charming stationery illustrations, craftsmanship, sunlit feel and paper textures. Landscape aspect ratio exactly 2:1 so each of the eight cells is square. No readable text, no logo, no captions, no watermark.

`src/home.css` contains the illustration windows, card frames, and responsive treatment. `src/data/artwork.js` maps catalogue categories to the contact sheet. Replace the PNG files to update the art while retaining all interactive controls.
